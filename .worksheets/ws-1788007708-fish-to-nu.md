## Findings
- sops `--encrypt` with `--age` still loads `.sops.yaml` and errors
  "no matching creation rules found" when the plaintext is outside `secrets/`.
  Workaround: a temp-dir local `.sops.yaml` with a catch-all rule.
- Sidecar convention (`.sha256`) stores the **plaintext** content hash, not the
  `.enc` hash (confirmed against `pi-auth.json.sha256`). `check-secrets` only
  verifies decryptability, so the convention is not enforced by CI.
- post_deploy.bash generic loop now skips every `.enc` (all handled by
  `app_secret`); harmless dead loop left in place.
- `bootstrap.sh` already installs Nushell via pacman (Linux) and Brewfile
  (macOS line 83); `fish` stays in Brewfile as the installed fallback.
- ⚠️ SECURITY (transcript leak): two separate incidents printed plaintext
